Case method:

  1. Strip URLs, HTML tags, punctuation — lowercase everything
  2. Remove stopwords + short tokens, lemmatize survivors
  3. Vectorize with TF‑IDF (unigrams & bigrams, 1,500 terms)
  4. Score with a Logistic Regression model trained on the corpus
  5. Report the verdict, confidence, and the words that swung it
